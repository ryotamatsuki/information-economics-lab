## Stage 7: welfare

Crossed profiles maximize V and minimize TC under fixed primitives; this is a constrained benchmark, not unrestricted first best. Worst pure equilibrium loss relative to crossed: t/2 for 0<=t<=t_alpha, zero for t_alpha<t<t_gamma, V_crossed-V_portfolio for t>=t_gamma. Best equilibrium efficient throughout. This is an equilibrium correspondence comparison, not actual policy trajectory. In portfolio-sorted equilibrium, moving A1 alone increases investment share .05532334 -> .12095095 but loses product profit .1 ->0 at t=.2; total .15532334 -> .12095095. Thus technically correct model not sufficient for independent economic contribution. Preserve as benchmark/research-note candidate, not an approved manuscript.
